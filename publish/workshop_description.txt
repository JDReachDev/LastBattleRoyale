[h1]Last Battle Royale 2.0[/h1]
A complete Battle Royale gamemode for Garry's Mod: drop in, loot weapons and armor, team up with your squad and survive the shrinking zone until only one is left.
Based on LAST STAND by Kanade, but almost completely rewritten.

[b]Source code (open source):[/b] [url=https://github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale]github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale[/url]

[h2]What's new in 2.0[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]New zone[/b]: the old sphere is gone. Now it is a glowing wall that follows the terrain and fades upward, with a light on the ground and a storm effect (fog, grey colors) when you are outside. No more "hole" in the sky from far away
[*][b]New HUD[/b] in battle royale style: shield and health bars, compass with zone and teammates, zone timer, kills, players alive, squad panel
[*][b]New menus[/b]: TAB scoreboard, F3 class menu (stats, loadout, level and unlocks) and F4 squad menu (invite and remove players with the mouse)
[*][b]In-game map editor (F8)[/b]: set up any map without writing code, configs saved as text files you can share
[*][b]Loot editor[/b]: choose in game which weapons, items and vehicles spawn and how often, from the addons you have installed
[*][b]Interaction prompts[/b]: look at a weapon, item, vehicle or supply drop and the screen tells you what [b]E[/b] does ([b][E] PICK UP M4A1[/b], [b][E] ENTER[/b]...)
[*][b]Safe spawns[/b]: players and loot no longer get stuck in floors or walls, and the existing maps keep working as before
[*][b]Much better performance on big maps[/b] (gm_fork, gm_valley): far loot and parked vehicles are not sent or drawn, lighter zone and outlines
[*][b]Settings renamed[/b] from lst_* to lbr_* (the old names still work)
[*]Lots of bug fixes
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shrinking safe zone[/b]: a glowing wall that follows the terrain, storm effect and damage outside, synced for every player
[*][b]Random loot[/b] every round: CW 2.0 weapons, attachments, medkits, adrenaline, armor, ghillie suit, ammo
[*][b]Vehicles[/b]: cars (SCars) and helicopters (WAC) around the map
[*][b]Events[/b]: supply airdrops, optional nuclear strike and implosion bomb
[*][b]Squads[/b] of up to 4 players with teammate markers on compass and screen
[*][b]9 classes[/b] unlocked by levelling up
[*][b]Fortnite-style HUD[/b]: shield and health bars, compass with zone and teammates, zone timer, kills, alive players, squad panel
[*][b]In-game map editor[/b]: configure ANY map without writing code
[*][b]Safe spawns[/b]: players and loot never get stuck in floors or walls
[*][b]Optimized for big maps[/b] (gm_fork, gm_valley): far loot and vehicles are not sent / drawn, lighter zone and outlines
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Lobby[/b]: everyone waits in the lobby until there are enough players (2 by default). Use this time to pick your class (F3) and make a squad (F4)
[*][b]Preparing[/b]: a countdown starts (30 s), then all players are spread around the map
[*][b]Loot[/b]: weapons can't be used for the first 30 s: run, find weapons, armor, medkits and vehicles
[*][b]Zone[/b]: the safe zone shrinks over time. Outside it you lose health and the screen gets stormy. Follow the zone marker on the compass and the timer on the top right
[*][b]Win[/b]: the last player (or the last squad) alive wins the round. After a few seconds everyone goes back to the lobby for the next round
[/olist]
Kills and wins give EXP to the class you are using. Level up classes to unlock new ones (F3 shows what each class needs). Dead players can spectate the others.

[h2]How to start a server[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Listen server[/b]: subscribe to this addon and to all the required items, open Garry's Mod, choose the [b]Last Battle Royale[/b] gamemode (bottom right of the main menu), pick one of the supported maps and start with 2+ player slots. The settings are in the Start New Game menu
[*][b]Dedicated server[/b]: use [b]+gamemode lastbattleroyale[/b] and a Workshop collection with this addon, the required items and your maps ([b]+host_workshop_collection[/b])
[*][b]Other maps[/b]: configure them in game with the map editor (F8), see below
[/list]

[h2]Keys[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]TAB[/b] - scoreboard (click for the mouse, right click a player to mute or open the profile)
[*][b]F3[/b] - class menu: stats, loadout, level and what unlocks each class
[*][b]F4[/b] - squad menu: invite players, remove mates ([b]F6[/b] decline / [b]F7[/b] accept an invite)
[*][b]Q[/b] - drop your active weapon
[*][b]E[/b] - pick up weapons, use items, enter vehicles, open supply drops (a prompt shows what you are looking at)
[*][b]E (hold) + Left Click[/b] - throw grenades (with a CW 2.0 weapon)
[*][b]F8[/b] - [i]admins:[/i] map editor
[/list]

[h2]Map editor (F8)[/h2]
Join the map as admin between rounds and press [b]F8[/b] (or type [b]lbr_editor[/b] in console). You fly around and see every point already placed.
[list]
[*][b]Left click[/b] place a point / [b]Right click[/b] remove it
[*][b]Mouse wheel / 1-8[/b] choose what to place: player spawns, lobby spawns, loot, cars, helicopters, zone centers, nuke targets, supply drops
[*][b]R[/b] place where you aim / at your position - [b]E[/b] go to next point - [b]Z[/b] undo
[*][b]Q[/b] menu: save, zone size, loot amount, player models, copy / import, [b]Loot and vehicles[/b]
[/list]
The panel tells you what is still missing for the map to be playable. Configs are saved in:
[code]garrysmod/data/lastbattleroyale/maps/<mapname>.txt[/code]
You can share these files: drop one in that folder and the map is ready. The configs of the supported maps are created there automatically, so you can edit them too ("Reset" restores the original).

[b]Loot and vehicles[/b] (F8, then Q): pick which weapons, items, cars and helicopters spawn, how likely each one is (weight), disable or add anything installed. Saved in [b]garrysmod/data/lastbattleroyale/loot.txt[/b] (all maps).

[h2]Server settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]lbr_min_players[/b] - players needed to start (default 2)
[*][b]lbr_zone_speed[/b] - zone shrink speed (default 0.75)
[*][b]lbr_zone_quality[/b] - client: zone wall quality, 3 high, 2 medium, 1 low, 0 invisible
[*][b]lbr_outline[/b] - client: outline on loot (1/0)
[*][b]lbr_zone_view_distance[/b] - client: how far the zone wall is drawn (default 9000)
[*][b]lbr_event_nuke / lbr_event_implosion[/b] - nuke and implosion events (1/0)
[*][b]lbr_cull_loot_distance[/b] - loot farther than this is not sent to players (default 4000, 0 = off)
[*][b]lbr_cull_vehicle_distance[/b] - client: parked vehicles farther than this are not drawn (default 8000)
[*][b]lbr_outline_distance[/b] - client: max loot outline distance (default 1500)
[*][b]lbr_editor_superadminonly[/b] - only superadmins can use the map editor (1/0)
[/list]
Old [b]lst_*[/b] names from previous versions still work.
Full list and documentation on [url=https://github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale]GitHub[/url].

[h2]Tips and problems[/h2]
[list]
[*]Weapons spawn with all attachments? Run [b]cw_disable_all_attachments_on_spawn 1[/b] on the server.
[*]Errors or lag? Disable the addons you don't need and delete [b]garrysmod/download/[/b] and [b]garrysmod/downloads/server/[/b] (old files from public servers).
[*]Low FPS on big maps? Lower [b]lbr_outline_distance[/b] and [b]lbr_zone_quality[/b].
[*]Keep nuke and implosion disabled for a better experience.
[/list]
