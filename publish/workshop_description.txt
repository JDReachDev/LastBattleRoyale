[h1]Last Battle Royale 2.0[/h1]
A complete Battle Royale gamemode for Garry's Mod: drop in, loot weapons, attachments and armor, team up with your squad and survive the shrinking zone until only one is left.
Based on LAST STAND by Kanade, but almost completely rewritten.

[b]Support the project:[/b] [url=https://ko-fi.com/jdreach]Ko-fi[/url]
[b]Source code (open source):[/b] [url=https://github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale]github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale[/url]

[h2]What's new in 2.0[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Inventory[/b] (hold TAB): 4 equipment + 2 backpack slots (server: 1-6 + 0-6), weapon icons and ammo. Drag to move, mouse wheel / number keys switch instantly. [b]Right click[/b]: drop a weapon or [b]unload a full magazine[/b] in an ammo box: anyone with a weapon of the same type (pistol / SMG, rifle, sniper, shotgun) takes it with E and gets one magazine of their own weapon
[*][b]Map (M)[/b]: the whole map from above with an A-H / 1-8 grid, you and your squad, the safe zone and its timer, supply drops, nuke target. Click to place a marker for your squad (also on compass and screen)
[*][b]Attachments are loot[/b]: attachment kits have their own loot category, about one kit every 3 weapons (set per map in the editor). In the C menu the missing ones are red
[*][b]New zone[/b]: a glowing wall with a storm effect outside. It waits 2 minutes before shrinking and always starts outside the map. Inside it your health regenerates up to 20
[*][b]New HUD and menus[/b]: shield and health bars, compass with zone and teammates, squad panel, TAB menu with stats, F3 classes, F4 squad
[*][b]Loot is easy to spot[/b]: outline and a small light beam on loot close by and in sight. The screen tells you what E does
[*][b]Supply drops[/b] land only inside the safe zone
[*][b]Map vote[/b] at the end of the match, admins change map with F2
[*][b]In-game map editor and loot editor[/b] (F8): set up any map and choose what spawns, no code needed
[*][b]Much better performance on big maps[/b], safe spawns, settings renamed from lst_* to lbr_* (old names still work), many bug fixes
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Random loot every round: CW 2.0 weapons, attachments, medkits, adrenaline, armor, ghillie suit, ammo
[*]Vehicles: cars (SCars) and helicopters (WAC)
[*]Events: supply airdrops, optional nuclear strike and implosion (built in, no extra addons)
[*]Squads of up to 4 players
[*]Classes with their own loadout, unlocked by levelling up
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Lobby[/b]: wait for enough players (2 by default), pick a class (F3) and make a squad (F4)
[*][b]Preparing[/b]: after a 30 s countdown everyone is spread around the map
[*][b]Loot[/b]: no attacks for the first 30 s. Pick up with [b]E[/b], hold TAB to sort your inventory
[*][b]Zone[/b]: after 2 minutes the zone starts shrinking. Outside it you lose health: follow the compass, the timer or the map ([b]M[/b])
[*][b]Win[/b]: the last player (or squad) alive wins, then back to the lobby or a map vote
[/olist]
Kills and wins give EXP to your class: level up to unlock new classes. Dead players can spectate.

[h2]Keys[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]TAB (hold)[/b] - scoreboard, inventory, stats
[*][b]M[/b] - map. [b]Left click[/b] places a marker for your squad, [b]right click[/b] removes it
[*][b]Mouse wheel / number keys[/b] - switch slot (the last one is melee)
[*][b]E[/b] - pick up weapons and ammo, use items, enter vehicles, open supply drops
[*][b]Q[/b] - drop your active weapon
[*][b]C[/b] - CW 2.0 attachments: press the category number to mount the ones you found
[*][b]E (hold) + Left Click[/b] - throw grenades
[*][b]F3[/b] class menu / [b]F4[/b] squad menu ([b]F6[/b] decline / [b]F7[/b] accept an invite)
[*][b]F2[/b] / [b]F8[/b] / [b]!start[/b] - [i]admins:[/i] change map / map editor / start now (even alone)
[/list]

[h2]How to start a server[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Listen server[/b]: subscribe to this addon and the required items, choose [b]Last Battle Royale[/b] (bottom right of the main menu), pick a supported map, 2+ player slots. Settings are in the Start New Game menu
[*][b]Dedicated server[/b]: [b]+gamemode lastbattleroyale[/b] and a Workshop collection ([b]+host_workshop_collection[/b])
[/list]

[h2]Supported maps[/h2]
rp_pripyat_cop (the best one), rp_city17_build210, gm_marsh, rp_wasteland, gm_fork, gm_valley, rp_ineu_pass_v1e. Any other map can be configured in game.

[h2]Map editor (F8)[/h2]
As admin between rounds press [b]F8[/b]: you fly around and see every point already placed.
[list]
[*][b]Left click[/b] place / [b]Right click[/b] remove - [b]Wheel / 1-8[/b] category (player spawns, lobby, loot, cars, helicopters, zone centers, nuke targets, supply drops)
[*][b]R[/b] at aim / at your position - [b]E[/b] next point - [b]Z[/b] undo - [b]WASD[/b] fly, [b]SHIFT[/b] faster, [b]CTRL[/b] slower
[*][b]M[/b] map from above with every point: wheel / 0-8 all or one category, left click flies there, right click removes
[*][b]Q[/b] menu: save, zone size and delay, loot amount, player models, copy / import, [b]Loot and vehicles[/b]
[/list]
Configs are in [b]garrysmod/data/lastbattleroyale/maps/<mapname>.txt[/b]: share them, drop one there and the map is ready. Loot tables: [b]garrysmod/data/lastbattleroyale/loot.txt[/b].

[list]
[*][b]lbr_min_players[/b] (2) - [b]lbr_inv_equipment / lbr_inv_backpack[/b] (4 / 2)
[*][b]lbr_time_preparing / lbr_time_weapons_hot / lbr_time_postround[/b] (30 / 30 / 15)
[*][b]lbr_zone_speed[/b] (0.75) - [b]lbr_zone_delay[/b] (120) - [b]lbr_zone_heal[/b] (1)
[*][b]lbr_mapvote[/b] (1) - [b]lbr_mapvote_rounds / _time / _maps[/b] (1 / 20 / 8)
[*][b]lbr_event_nuke / lbr_event_implosion[/b] (0 / 0) - [b]lbr_exp_scale[/b] (1) - [b]lbr_css_armor[/b] (1)
[*][b]lbr_cw_spawn_attachments[/b] (0) - [b]lbr_cull_loot_distance[/b] (4000) - [b]lbr_editor_superadminonly[/b]
[*]Client: [b]lbr_zone_quality[/b] (3), [b]lbr_outline / lbr_loot_beam / lbr_outline_distance[/b] (1 / 1 / 1500)
[/list]
