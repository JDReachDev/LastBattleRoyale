[h1]Last Battle Royale 2.0[/h1]
A complete Battle Royale gamemode for Garry's Mod: drop in, loot weapons, attachments and armor, team up with your squad and survive the shrinking zone until only one is left.
Based on LAST STAND by Kanade, but almost completely rewritten.

[b]Source code (open source):[/b] [url=https://github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale]github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale[/url]

[h2]What's new in 2.0[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Inventory[/b]: 5 equipment slots + 3 backpack slots by default (the server can set 1-6 + 0-6). Hold TAB to see it with weapon icons and ammo, drag to move, right click to drop the weapon or unload a full magazine in a box (anyone with a weapon of the same type, pistol / SMG, rifle, sniper or shotgun, takes it with E and gets one magazine of that weapon). Mouse wheel and the number keys switch instantly, melee always last
[*][b]New TAB menu[/b]: scoreboard, inventory, your stats (class, level, EXP, kills) and buttons for classes, squad and map
[*][b]Map[/b] (M or the TAB button): the whole map seen from above, up to its borders, on any map (also the ones you configure yourself)
[list]
[*]Battleship grid (A-H / 1-8) to call positions to your squad
[*]You and your teammates, even when they are far away
[*]Safe zone with its timer: outside it the map is darker and striped, and a line shows the way back in
[*]Supply drops still to open, the nuke target, where your teammates fell
[*][b]Click to place a marker[/b] for your squad: it also shows on the compass and on screen with the distance (right click removes it)
[/list]
[*][b]Attachments are loot[/b]: you start with none and find attachment kits around the map. In the C menu the ones you don't have are red
[*][b]New zone[/b]: the old sphere is gone. Now it is a glowing wall, brightest at eye height and fading up and down, with a storm effect (fog, grey colors) when you are outside
[*][b]New HUD[/b]: shield and health bars, compass with zone and teammates, zone timer, kills, players alive, squad panel. One clean style for HUD, menus and messages
[*][b]New menus[/b]: F3 class menu (stats, loadout, level and unlocks), F4 squad menu (invite and remove players with the mouse)
[*][b]Map vote[/b] at the end of the match, only between maps ready to play. Admins can change map with F2
[*][b]In-game map editor (F8)[/b]: set up any map without writing code, configs saved as text files you can share
[*][b]Loot editor[/b]: choose in game which weapons, items and vehicles spawn and how often, from the addons you have installed
[*][b]Interaction prompts[/b]: the screen tells you what [b]E[/b] does ([b][E] PICK UP M4A1[/b], [b][E] ENTER[/b]...)
[*][b]Safe spawns[/b]: players and loot no longer get stuck in floors or walls
[*][b]Much better performance on big maps[/b] (gm_fork, gm_valley): far loot and parked vehicles are not sent or drawn
[*][b]Settings renamed[/b] from lst_* to lbr_* (the old names still work)
[*]Many bug fixes
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shrinking safe zone[/b] with storm effect and damage outside, synced for every player
[*][b]Random loot[/b] every round: CW 2.0 weapons, attachments, medkits, adrenaline, armor, ghillie suit, ammo
[*][b]Vehicles[/b]: cars (SCars) and helicopters (WAC) around the map
[*][b]Events[/b]: supply airdrops, optional nuclear strike and implosion bomb
[*][b]Squads[/b] of up to 4 players with teammate markers on compass, screen and map
[*][b]Classes[/b] with their own loadout, unlocked by levelling up
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Lobby[/b]: everyone waits until there are enough players (2 by default). Pick your class (F3) and make a squad (F4)
[*][b]Preparing[/b]: a countdown starts (30 s), then all players are spread around the map
[*][b]Loot[/b]: weapons can't be used for the first 30 s: find weapons, attachments, armor, medkits and vehicles. Pick up with [b]E[/b], hold TAB to sort your inventory
[*][b]Zone[/b]: the safe zone shrinks over time. Outside it you lose health and the screen gets stormy. Follow the zone marker on the compass and the timer on the top right, or open the map with [b]M[/b]
[*][b]Win[/b]: the last player (or squad) alive wins. Then everyone goes back to the lobby, or votes the next map
[/olist]
Kills and wins give EXP to your class. Level up to unlock new classes (F3 shows what each one needs). Dead players can spectate.

[h2]How to start a server[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Listen server[/b]: subscribe to this addon and all the required items, choose the [b]Last Battle Royale[/b] gamemode (bottom right of the main menu), pick a supported map and start with 2+ player slots. Settings are in the Start New Game menu
[*][b]Dedicated server[/b]: [b]+gamemode lastbattleroyale[/b] and a Workshop collection with this addon, the required items and your maps ([b]+host_workshop_collection[/b])
[*][b]Other maps[/b]: configure them in game with the map editor (F8)
[/list]

[h2]Keys[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]TAB (hold)[/b] - scoreboard, inventory (drag to move, right click to drop the weapon or unload a magazine), stats
[*][b]M[/b] - map of the whole map: you, your squad, the safe zone, supply drops, nuke and the A-H / 1-8 grid (also from the MAP button in TAB). [b]Left click[/b] on it places a marker for your squad, [b]right click[/b] removes it
[*][b]Mouse wheel / 1-5[/b] - switch between the equipment slots (6: melee; with more or fewer slots the keys follow)
[*][b]E[/b] - pick up weapons, use items, enter vehicles, open supply drops
[*][b]Q[/b] - drop your active weapon
[*][b]C[/b] - CW 2.0 customization: press the category number (1, 2, 3...) to mount the attachments you found
[*][b]E (hold) + Left Click[/b] - throw grenades (with a CW 2.0 weapon)
[*][b]F3[/b] - class menu / [b]F4[/b] - squad menu ([b]F6[/b] decline / [b]F7[/b] accept an invite)
[*][b]F2[/b] - [i]admins:[/i] change map: ready ones or new ones to configure
[*][b]F8[/b] - [i]admins:[/i] map editor
[*][b]!start[/b] or [b]TAB > START NOW[/b] - [i]admins:[/i] start now (even alone to test)
[/list]

[h2]Supported maps[/h2]
rp_pripyat_cop (the best one), rp_city17_build210, gm_marsh, rp_wasteland, gm_fork, gm_valley, rp_ineu_pass_v1e. Any other map can be configured in game.

[h2]Map editor (F8)[/h2]
As admin between rounds press [b]F8[/b] (or [b]lbr_editor[/b] in console): you fly around and see every point already placed.
[list]
[*][b]Left click[/b] place a point / [b]Right click[/b] remove it
[*][b]Mouse wheel / 1-8[/b] choose what to place: player spawns, lobby spawns, loot, cars, helicopters, zone centers, nuke targets, supply drops
[*][b]R[/b] place where you aim / at your position - [b]E[/b] next point - [b]Z[/b] undo
[*][b]Q[/b] menu: save, zone size, loot amount, player models, copy / import, [b]Loot and vehicles[/b]
[/list]
The panel tells you what is still missing. Configs are saved in [b]garrysmod/data/lastbattleroyale/maps/<mapname>.txt[/b]: share them, drop one in that folder and the map is ready. The supported maps are there too, so you can edit them ("Reset" restores the original).

[b]Loot and vehicles[/b] (F8, then Q): pick which weapons, items, cars and helicopters spawn, how likely each one is, extra ammo on pickup. Saved in [b]garrysmod/data/lastbattleroyale/loot.txt[/b] (all maps).

[h2]Server settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]lbr_min_players[/b] - players needed to start (default 2)
[*][b]lbr_inv_equipment / lbr_inv_backpack[/b] - equipment slots (1-6) and backpack slots (0-6), default 5 / 3, applied in the lobby
[*][b]lbr_time_preparing / lbr_time_weapons_hot[/b] - lobby countdown and no-attack time (default 30 / 30)
[*][b]lbr_zone_speed[/b] - zone shrink speed (default 0.75)
[*][b]lbr_mapvote[/b] - map vote at the end of the match (1/0), [b]lbr_mapvote_rounds[/b] matches before the vote (default 1)
[*][b]lbr_event_nuke / lbr_event_implosion[/b] - nuke and implosion events (1/0)
[*][b]lbr_cw_spawn_attachments[/b] - 1 = all attachments on spawn, as plain CW 2.0
[*][b]lbr_cull_loot_distance[/b] - loot farther than this is not sent to players (default 4000, 0 = off)
[*][b]lbr_editor_superadminonly[/b] - only superadmins can use the map editor (1/0)
[*][b]lbr_zone_quality[/b] - client: zone wall, 3 high, 2 medium, 1 low, 0 invisible
[*][b]lbr_outline / lbr_loot_beam / lbr_outline_distance[/b] - client: outline and light beam on loot (1/0) and their max distance (default 1500)
[*][b]lbr_zone_view_distance / lbr_cull_vehicle_distance[/b] - client: how far the zone and parked vehicles are drawn (9000 / 8000)
[/list]
Old [b]lst_*[/b] names still work. Full list on [url=https://github.com/JDReachDev/LastBattleRoyale]GitHub[/url].

[h2]Tips and problems[/h2]
[list]
[*]Errors or lag? Disable the addons you don't need and delete [b]garrysmod/download/[/b] and [b]garrysmod/downloads/server/[/b] (old files from public servers).
[*]Low FPS on big maps? Lower [b]lbr_outline_distance[/b] and [b]lbr_zone_quality[/b].
[*]Keep nuke and implosion disabled for a better experience.
[/list]
